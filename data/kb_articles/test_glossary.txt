Technical Support Glossary

ELD (Electronic Logging Device):
A device that automatically records a driver's driving time and other aspects of Hours of Service (HOS) records. Connects directly to the vehicle's engine to track vehicle movement.

HOS (Hours of Service):
Federal regulations that govern the working hours of anyone operating a commercial motor vehicle. Key components include:
- 11-Hour Driving Limit: May drive up to 11 hours after 10 consecutive hours off duty
- 14-Hour Limit: May not drive beyond the 14th consecutive hour after coming on duty
- Rest Break: Must take a 30-minute break when driving for more than 8 cumulative hours

GPS Tracking:
Real-time vehicle location monitoring system. Features include:
- Live Position Updates: Current vehicle location updated every 2-3 minutes
- Geofencing: Create virtual boundaries to monitor vehicle entry/exit
- Route History: Review past vehicle movements and stops

Vehicle Diagnostics:
Engine data monitoring system that:
- Reads engine fault codes
- Monitors fuel consumption
- Tracks engine hours and odometer readings
- Reports maintenance needs

Driver Status:
Different duty status options in the ELD system:
- On Duty (Driving): When operating the vehicle
- On Duty (Not Driving): Working but not driving
- Off Duty: Not working
- Sleeper Berth: Resting in vehicle's sleeper compartment

System Requirements:
Minimum specifications for running the tracking software:
- Internet: Stable broadband connection (minimum 5 Mbps)
- Browser: Chrome 90+, Firefox 88+, or Edge 90+
- Mobile App: iOS 13+ or Android 8+
- Data: 500MB monthly data plan per vehicle

Common Issues:
- No Data Connection: Check cellular signal and device power
- Login Failed: Verify credentials and internet connection
- GPS Signal Loss: Ensure clear view of sky
- Data Not Syncing: Check internet connection and refresh application
